Why the MARS file differs

MARS archives ETADOT as spectral T639 coefficients. For a 0.25° grid it synthesises the full field on the regular Gaussian grid N360 (1440×720, longitudes coinciding with the 0.25° points) and then interpolates linearly in latitude to your latitudes. These are the documented MARS defaults (truncation=AUTO, intgrid=AUTO, interpolation=linear). Repeating exactly this reproduces the MARS 0.25° η̇, divergence, LNSP, U and V to 0.00002 %.

Linear interpolation between rows damps the shortest waves. η̇ and divergence are affected because 3–38 % of their variance sits above wavenumber 320, T, U and V are not. Nothing is wrong with the gridded file, and MARS does not recompute η̇. Our exact evaluation is closer to the model's own field. --gridding mars repeats MARS's step for consistency with MARS-route data; it is not better data.
