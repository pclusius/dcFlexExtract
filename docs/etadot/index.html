How ETADOT gets into the hourly files

FLEXPART needs the vertical velocity as η̇·∂p/∂η on half levels. dcFlexExtract has two ways to obtain the η̇ it starts from, and one shared step that turns it into that field.

What differs between the routes

  • Gridding. The model η̇ is a T639 spectral field. MARS puts it on the 0.25° grid by synthesising it on a Gaussian grid and interpolating linearly in latitude, which smooths the shortest waves (about 8 % in η̇). The mixed route evaluates the sums exactly at your grid points by default. --gridding mars repeats MARS's steps, only for consistency with older MARS-route data; it is not better data.
  • Levels used. Continuity needs the divergence on all 137 levels, also when only levels 60–137 are written. The winds are needed where ΔB ≠ 0 (levels 54–137 for the L137 coefficients).
  • Queue. The MARS route waits in the MARS queue. The mixed route reads ARCO over HTTPS and does not queue.

How close the two come is on the sanity checks page.
